What do ply and GSM mean?

Single-ply means each napkin has one layer of tissue paper. GSM means grams per square metre and describes paper weight; it does not by itself determine softness.

As a starting point, ask us about 15, 17, 18 or 20 GSM grades. Availability and suitability must be confirmed with the paper supplier and tested on the machine. Higher grades such as 70 GSM are not typical tissue-napkin grades, so they are not listed as standard single-ply napkin options.

Please note: All grades, finishes, ply, dimensions, printing, packaging and availability are subject to confirmation in the quotation for each order.
